About SIDBI

India’s apex Institution for MSME development

Small Industries Development Bank of India was established in 1990 by an Act of Parliament as the principal financial institution for the promotion, financing and development of India's MSME sector. SIDBI supports MSMEs through financing, ecosystem development, and technology-driven lending solutions.

The Bank executes its mandate through:

  • Indirect lending for wider MSME reach via financial institutions
  • Direct lending to bridge credit gaps with scalable, innovative products
  • Fund of Funds to support startups and boost entrepreneurship
  • Promotion and Development initiatives offering credit-plus support for holistic MSME growth and
  • Facilitator role as a nodal agency for government MSME schemes.

About Regional Rural Bank

Driving financial inclusion across rural India

Regional Rural Banks were established under the Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976 to strengthen institutional credit in rural India. Since their inception in 1975, RRBs have played a crucial role in supporting agriculture, small businesses, and rural entrepreneurship.

Regional Rural Banks were established under the provision of RRBs Act, 1976 with an objective to create an alternative channel to the cooperative credit structure so as to facilitate an expanded bandwidth of institutional credit for agriculture and rural sector. It started with 5 RRBs in the year 1975, first RRB was setup on 02nd October, 1975 under the provisions of the Ordinance promulgated on 26th September, 1975, for the purpose of development of agriculture, trade, commerce, industry and other productive activities in the rural areas, credit and other facilities, particularly to small and marginal farmers, agricultural labourers, artisans and small entrepreneurs, and for matters connected therewith and incidental thereto. RRBs carry the basic mandate of ensuring rural development and foster financial inclusion.

The framework behind the Co-Lending Model

Under the Co-Lending Arrangement framework introduced by the Reserve Bank of India, regulated financial institutions can jointly fund loans while sharing risk and returns.

RRBs serve as the originating institution and take responsibility for customer onboarding.

2

SIDBI co-funds the portfolio

They provide frameworks and digital systems that help RRBs originate and manage MSME loans.

3

Sharing revenue and risks

The model creates a synergistic partnership that leverages both institutions to support MSMEs.
